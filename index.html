    How to play

    Every day there's one new top 10. Name as many entries as you can.

    1. Type a guess. If it's on the list, it lands in its spot on the board.
    2. You have 5 misses. A wrong guess costs one. Small typos still count, and repeats or vague answers ("Bush", "Star Wars") are free.
    3. Just outside? If your guess is #11–13, you'll hear how close you were, but it still counts as a miss.
    4. One free clue shows the first letters of the lowest unfound answer.
    5. 1 point per answer, so 10 is a perfect game. A new list arrives at midnight.
